Know Your Rights as a Debtor

National Credit Act (NCA)
  • Right to be declared over-indebted
  • Right to apply for debt review (Section 86)
  • Creditors cannot take legal action during debt review
Your Rights
  • Right to affordable payment — cannot be forced to pay what you cannot afford
  • Right to information — creditors must give full debt details
  • Right to privacy — no work calls without permission
Creditors CANNOT
  • Call before 8am or after 8pm
  • Call at work without permission
  • Refuse reasonable payment offer (NCA Section 86(11))
  • Harass or intimidate you
